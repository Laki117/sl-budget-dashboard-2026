Methodology & Sources

Data Pipeline

Figures extracted from official Budget Speech and Activity Estimate PDFs published by the Sri Lanka Treasury. Transformed via a reproducible pipeline (scripts/build_data.mjs) with zero interpolation.

Coverage

Ministry allocations cover 16 ministries from the Activity Estimate. This is a subset of total government expenditure. Headline fiscal metrics cover the full national budget.

Sources

All source URLs and retrieval timestamps documented in sources.md. Primary: Budget Speech 2026, Approved Estimates, and Activity Estimates from treasury.gov.lk.
